The house

A natural white brick and cedar contemporary from 1983, set back on a wooded acre at the end of a cul-de-sac. Inside, cedar walls and ceilings, an open oak stair and tall windows carry the woods into the house; the main floor opens to the south, so the mountains are in view from the living room, the kitchen and the deck.

The house has been fully rewired, and the lower level is completely finished. The interior has been freshly painted throughout and is shown empty, with three rooms virtually staged and labelled as such.

The grounds

About an acre of woods on a cul-de-sac, with a screened porch, a deck and a fire pit facing the trees.

02

Lower-level suite

The finished lower level has its own exterior entrance and a full bath. Its four bedrooms each have an egress window; the plan also shows a recreation room, an exercise room, a workshop and a mudroom.

Altogether the lower level adds 1,491 sq ft of finished space to the 3,814 sq ft above ground.

The view

The deck faces due south, toward Ellett. The ridgeline is visible from the house in every season, and the low western sun lights it from the side in the late afternoon.

06

Life in Blacksburg

Cedarview Drive is in Forest Hills, an established wooded neighbourhood of Blacksburg, a university town in the New River Valley and home to Virginia Tech and its Corporate Research Center. Downtown, the campus and the surrounding trails are all a short drive from the house.

LewisGale Hospital Montgomery is in Blacksburg; Carilion New River Valley Medical Center is in neighbouring Christiansburg.
