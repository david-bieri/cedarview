01

The house

A natural white brick and cedar contemporary from 1983, set back on a wooded acre at the end of a cul-de-sac. Inside, cedar walls and ceilings, an open oak stair and tall windows carry the woods into the house; the main floor opens to the south, so the mountains are in view from the living room, the kitchen and the deck.

The main level and the lower level have been rewired, and the lower level is completely finished. The interior has been freshly painted throughout and is shown empty, with eleven views virtually staged by the owners and labelled as such; every one can be toggled back to the photograph.

Rooms marked “virtually staged” have furniture added digitally; walls, floors, windows and the view are untouched.

Lower-level suite

The finished lower level has its own exterior entrance and a full bath. Its four bedrooms each have an egress window, two with walk-in closets; the plan also shows a recreation room, a mudroom, a utility room and a large storage and workshop room.

Altogether the lower level adds 1,491 sq ft of finished space to the 3,814 sq ft above ground.

Life in Blacksburg

Cedarview Drive is in Forest Hills, an established wooded neighbourhood on the south side of Blacksburg, a university town in the New River Valley and home to Virginia Tech and its Corporate Research Center.

The nearest parks and the neighbourhood pool are a walk from the door; the shops on South Main are a short ride; campus, downtown and the library are a few minutes by car or bike. LewisGale Hospital Montgomery is in Blacksburg; Carilion New River Valley Medical Center is in neighbouring Christiansburg.
